Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Incremendle is a hobby project run by one person, Nick DiRienzo, not a company. "We" on this page means me (and Claude).

The short version: no accounts, no cookies, no ads, and nothing that says who you are. Your progress stays in your browser.

In your browser

Saves, finished games, streaks, gold stars and sound settings live in your browser's local storage on this site. They never leave your device unless you copy a save code yourself. Clearing this site's data in your browser wipes them.

Page views

The site runs on Cloudflare, which also counts page views with its Web Analytics: which pages get visited, where visitors came from, and their browser and country. It uses no cookies and doesn't follow you around the web. Like any web host, Cloudflare sees your IP address to send you the pages. We don't keep request logs.

Kids

The games are for all ages, kids included. We never ask for a name, email or anything else that identifies you.

Questions

The whole site is open source, so you can read exactly what it does. Questions? Ask in the discussions on GitHub, or for anything private, email contact@incremendle.com. If this page changes, the date at the top will too.
