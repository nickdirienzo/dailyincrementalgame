Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Daily Incremental is a hobby project run by one person, Nick DiRienzo, not a company. "We" on this page means me (and Claude).

The short version: no accounts, no cookies, no ads, and nothing that says who you are. Your progress stays in your browser. We count anonymous plays and finishes to see which games people like so we can build better games.

In your browser

Saves, finished games, streaks, gold stars and sound settings live in your browser's local storage on this site. They never leave your device. Clearing this site's data in your browser wipes them.

What we count

Once you tap or press a key in a game, your browser sends us a small tally for that game every few minutes and when you leave:

  • a random id your browser makes up the first time, which isn't tied to your name or anything else about you
  • which game it is
  • how many times you've sat down to play it
  • how many seconds the game was on screen, and how many of those you were actively playing
  • whether you reached the ending
  • the country you're in, which Cloudflare works out from the connection

That's all. We don't store your IP address, and we never see your saves or anything you type. We use the totals to learn how long games really take and which kinds people finish, so future games get better.

Page views

The site runs on Cloudflare, which also counts page views with its Web Analytics: which pages get visited, where visitors came from, and their browser and country. It uses no cookies and doesn't follow you around the web. Like any web host, Cloudflare sees your IP address to send you the pages.

Keeping it

The counts sit in a database on our Cloudflare account, on servers that may be outside your country, and we keep them as long as the site runs. Nothing in them points back to you, so we can't look up or delete yours in particular. Clearing this site's data starts you fresh.

Opting out

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we don't count anything. Clearing this site's data also throws away your random id, and the next one is brand new.

We don't ask before counting because the counts are small and only used to make better games (EU and UK law calls this a "legitimate interest"). If you'd rather we didn't count you, opt out as above. And if you're in the EU or UK and think we're mishandling your data, you can report us to your country's privacy regulator.

Sharing

We don't sell or share any of this, and would only hand it over if the law required it. If the site ever changes hands, this policy goes with it.

Kids

The games are for everyone, and we don't knowingly collect anything personal from anyone, kids included.

Questions

The whole site is open source, so you can read exactly what it does. Questions? Ask in the discussions on GitHub, or for anything private, email contact@dailyincrementalgame.com. If this page changes, the date at the top will too.
